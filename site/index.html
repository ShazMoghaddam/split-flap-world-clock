Features

  • Your cities

    Up to ten from 290 cities around the world. Your home city comes from your device and stays on top; drag the rest into any order.

  • Feels mechanical

    Tiles flip when they change, idle tiles twitch now and then, and F sends a cascade across the whole board.

  • Sounds right

    Three flap sounds made live in the browser: heavy mechanical, plastic rattle and classic airport. Volume and mute included.

  • Easy on the eyes at night

    The board drifts from a cool daylight white to a dim amber through the evening, and back again by morning.

  • Fits any screen

    From a small laptop to a 4K monitor, every row fits without scrolling, even with ten cities.

  • Private and offline

    One self-contained file with the fonts built in. No accounts, no tracking, and nothing leaves your device.

How to use it

  1. Open the clock

    Open it here in Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox. It starts with your home city and six others.

  2. Go full screen

    Move the mouse or tap to show the controls, then press the full screen button or Shift + F. The screen stays awake while the clock is showing, where the browser allows it.

  3. Make it yours

    The gear opens Preferences: search for cities, pick one of nine accent colours and choose a flap sound. Everything is saved in your browser.
