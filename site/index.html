Features

  • Your cities

    Up to ten from 290 cities around the world. Your home city comes from your device and stays on top; drag the rest into any order.

  • Real split flaps

    Each change falls over in two hinged halves, like an airport board. Idle tiles twitch now and then, and F sends one smooth wave across the board.

  • Day and night at a glance

    A sun or moon on every row, its time difference from home, and clear dates like "MON 02 NOV". The board warms to amber through the evening.

  • Your colours

    Pick any colour for the characters and the accent, start from a preset, and switch the backlit glow on or off.

  • Sounds right

    Three flap sounds made live in the browser: heavy mechanical, plastic rattle and classic airport. Volume and mute included.

  • Any screen, fully offline

    The board grows with your screen, up to 4K, and still fits ten cities. One file with the fonts built in: no accounts, no tracking.

How to use it

  1. Open the clock

    Open it here in Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox. It starts with your home city and six others, and a short hint shows the shortcuts on your first visit.

  2. Go full screen

    Move the mouse or tap to show the controls, then press the full screen button or Shift + F. The screen stays awake while the clock is showing, where the browser allows it.

  3. Make it yours

    The gear opens Settings. Tap cities to add or remove them, pick any colour for the characters and the accent, and choose a flap sound. Everything is saved in your browser.
